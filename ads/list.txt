MONETIZATION INVENTORY

FORMAT: [Location] -> [Ad Type] (Implementation Details)

1. GLOBAL / LIFECYCLE
   - App Open Ad: Shown on app resume/foregrounding.
     * Suppression Policy: Disabled on (auth), onboarding, terms, privacy, and weather screens.
   - Interstitial Ad: Preloaded globally.
     * Usage: Manual triggers during high-value transitions.

2. HOME SCREEN
   - Banner Ad: Anchored Adaptive Banner.
     * Placement: Positioned directly under the HomeHeader (full-width, no padding).

3. WEATHER SCREEN (Quiet Monetization Policy)
   - Native Ad Card: Medium Rectangle (300x250).
     * Placement: Injected between WeatherHero (Current) and WeatherStats.
   - Banner Ad: Anchored Adaptive Banner.
     * Placement: Positioned at the bottom of the ScrollView (full-width).
   - App Open Ad: SUPPRESSED (Utility focus).

4. CATEGORY LISTING SCREEN
   - Native Ad Card: Medium Rectangle (300x250).
     * Placement: Injected into the FlatList every 6 items (All tab only).

5. DRAWER / NAVIGATION
   - Rewarded Ad: High-value video ad.
     * Trigger: "Watch Ad" menu item in the CustomDrawerContent.

6. CORE COMPONENTS (ads/components)
   - BannerAd.tsx: Handles loading state, adaptive sizing, and themed placeholders.
   - NativeAd.tsx: Handles loading state, "Sponsored" branding, and themed card styling.
